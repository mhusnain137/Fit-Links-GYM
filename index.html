OFFICIAL TECHNICAL PROPOSAL & COMMERCIAL QUOTATION

Automated Turnstile Attendance & Cafe Management System

Client: FITLINKS Athletic Club, Faisalabad • Terminal: ZKTeco uFace800 Plus & Turnstiles • Document Version: 1.0 (Final)

Project Objective

FITLINKS Gym ke liye ek complete automated system tayyar kiya ja raha hai jo aapki maujooda ZKTeco uFace800 Plus biometric machine aur turnstile gate ke sath directly link hoga. Iske tehat sirf active members ko gate se entry milegi, new members ke free trial duration ko client khud control kar sakega, aur gym ke Protein & Cafe Bar ke liye alag se staff login provide kiya jayega taake daily sales aur member tabs asani se manage hon.

Scope of Work & Deliverables (Point-to-Point)

01

Biometric Attendance & Turnstile Gate Sync (ZKTeco uFace800 Plus)

PKR 20,000
  • Gym ki existing ZKTeco uFace800 Plus machine ke sath local network (TCP/IP) ke zariye direct integration.
  • Member face ya fingerprint lagaye ga toh system < 0.3 second mein membership status verify karega.
  • Active Membership: System turnstile relay ko unlock signal bhejega aur gate open ho jayega.
  • Expired / Unpaid: Gate bilkul LOCKED rahega, turnstile rotate nahi hoga, aur receptionist screen par alert aayega.
02

Membership Management & Configurable Free Trial Engine

PKR 15,000
  • Free Trial Control: Client/Admin khud control kar sakega ke new members ko kitne din ka free trial dena hai (e.g., 1 din, 3 din, ya 7 din).
  • Free trial period khatam hote hi system automatically member ka gate access block kar dega jab tak regular package activate na ho.
  • Morning vs Evening time-slot restrictions (e.g., Morning slot wale evening mein enter nahi ho sakein ge).
  • Member profile record with photo, phone number, emergency contact, aur package expiry date.
03

Dedicated Protein Bar & Cafe POS (Separate Staff Login)

PKR 15,000
  • Separate Login Portal: Barista / Cafe staff ke liye alag login screen — gym ke main financial aur member confidential data tak cafe staff ki access nahi hogi.
  • Rapid Touch POS menu: Protein shakes (Whey Isolate, Mass Gainer), pre-workout shots, smoothies, aur supplements.
  • Member Tab Auto-Debit: Member ke name par direct bill charge karna ya instant Cash / Card checkout.
  • Daily Cafe sales reports aur drink inventory tracking.
04

Central Admin Dashboard & Live Attendance Reporting

PKR 10,000
  • Real-time gym floor occupancy gauge (e.g., iss waqt gym mein kitne log workout kar rahe hain).
  • Hourly peak rush hours analysis (morning vs evening peak timing graph).
  • Daily consolidated revenue report: Membership collection + Cafe/Bar sales.
  • Exportable attendance log aur membership renewal due list.
05

Deployment, Staff Training & 15-Day Free Support / Minor Changes Warranty

INCLUDED FREE
  • FITLINKS Faisalabad gym branch par on-site local network setup aur turnstile testing.
  • Receptionist aur Cafe staff ki comprehensive usage training.
  • 15-Day Complete Warranty: System live hone ke baad 15 din ke andar agar koi bhi technical masla aata hai ya koi choti-moti changes/adjustments karwani hon, toh woh bina kisi extra charges ke completely free solve ki jayengi.
